■ 核心架构: HormonicBlockV2

输入 x -> [Embed + PosEnc] -> N x HormonicBlock -> [LayerNorm -> Output]

HormonicBlock 内部:
  1. CGL Field Evolution (核心)
     psi(t+dt) = psi(t) + dt * [R(psi) + D * Laplacian(psi)]
     R(psi) = alpha*psi - beta*|psi|^2*psi (反应项)
     Laplacian = 有限差分或DFT (扩散项)

  2. Hebbian Plasticity (可选)
     G <- G + eta_hebb * (x outer y) - eta_anti * (G * |G|)
     Prune & Regrow (BWO)

  3. Neuromodulation (辅助)
     - E/I Balance (侧抑制)
     - Sensory Feedback
     - Cross-Frequency Coupling
     - Energy Constraint
